# PrepMate — System Overview

## 1. Mission & Vision
**PrepMate** is an intelligent, multi-industry interview preparation platform designed to help job candidates practice real-time technical and situational interviews. The platform pairs candidates with an AI interviewer persona capable of dynamic question deepening, evidence verification, and contradiction detection. Candidates receive structured diagnostic performance feedback scored against the STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) methodology.

---

## 2. Supported Industry Domains & Job Families
PrepMate operates across 5 core job families with domain-specific question strategies and evaluation criteria:

| Industry Domain | Key Job Roles | Evaluation Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technology (IT & Software)** | Frontend, Backend, DevOps, Data Analyst, Solutions Architect | System architecture, concurrency, algorithmic design, performance metrics |
| **Marketing & Growth** | Performance Marketer, SEO Specialist, Brand Manager, Content Lead | Campaign CAC/LTV, attribution models, channel strategy, conversion rate |
| **Finance & Accounting** | Financial Analyst, Auditor, Risk Officer, Corporate Accountant | IFRS/GAAP standards, variance analysis, financial forecasting, valuation models |
| **Human Resources (HR)** | HR Generalist, Talent Acquisition, HRBP, Compensation & Benefits | Labor law compliance, conflict resolution, recruitment funnel, retention |
| **Business Analysis (BA)** | Business Analyst, Product Owner, Requirements Engineer | Requirement elicitation, BPMN workflows, gap analysis, stakeholder alignment |

---

## 3. Platform Capabilities
1. **Interactive Setup Wizard:** 4-step wizard for role specification, CV dropzone, target company input, and hardware readiness checking.
2. **Live Interview Room HUD:** Zoom-style virtual conference interface featuring live video camera feed, audio waveform, real-time timer, think-time countdown, and chat transcript.
3. **Adaptive Conversational AI:** Gemini-powered interviewer that analyzes answers in real time, generates contextual follow-ups, and detects unverified claims.
4. **Diagnostic STAR Report:** Comprehensive evaluation breakdown across 6 scoring dimensions with targeted strengths and improvement roadmaps.
5. **CV Analysis & Job Matches:** Automatic resume skill extraction and matching against current market job requisites.
6. **Career Roadmap:** Step-by-step career milestones and skill acquisition guides.
7. **Simulated Payment Gateway:** Multi-method checkout (MoMo, ZaloPay, Credit Card) with automated Pro subscription provisioning.
8. **Administration Console:** User management, role provisioning, subscription tier administration, and dynamic AI provider configuration.
